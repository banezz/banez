About Harsh Nayak (Banez)

Harsh Nayak, also known professionally and online by his moniker Banez, is a 16-year-old certified Google AI professional, cybersecurity enthusiast, gamer, and mountaineer originally from Chaibasa, Jharkhand, India (born 26 February 2010).
